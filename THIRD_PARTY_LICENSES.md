## NASA / U.S. government science sources

The physics page links to NASA pages and reports for Apollo radiation, orbital mechanics, cosmology, CMB, JWST and related topics. Those sources are cited for factual information. Their material remains subject to NASA/U.S. government media-use rules and any credit lines shown on the original page.

## CERN / Stanford / EPA / other science sources

The site links to CERN/Geant4, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, EPA resources and scientific papers for technical explanations. Linking/citation does not relicense their text, figures or other material.
